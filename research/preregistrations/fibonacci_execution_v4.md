# Fibonacci Execution V4 Preregistration

Date: 2026-08-17

## Question

After a causally confirmed impulse, a rejection near the 0.618 retracement,
and aligned EMA trend, does a non-chasing retest limit improve net expectancy?

## Fixed Variants

- 0.618034 limit with 1, 3, and 6 completed-bar TTLs.
- 0.650000 limit with a 3-bar TTL.
- 0.705000 practitioner-zone limit with a 3-bar TTL.
- 0.786000 deep-retracement limit with a 3-bar TTL.
- 0.550000 non-Fibonacci placebo with a 3-bar TTL.

Every order expires without conversion to market. The stop is beyond the
impulse origin by 0.05 ATR. The target is the prior impulse extreme. Same-bar
stop/target ambiguity is scored stop-first. Base round-trip cost is 2 bps and
the stress case doubles it.

## Locked Evaluation

- Development: 2020-2023.
- Selection: 2024.
- Final: 2025 onward, never used to choose the variant.
- External confirmation: QQQ and IWM final periods.
- One filled trade per symbol/session/variant maximum.

A variant is selectable only if development and selection each have at least
40 trades, positive net expectancy, and profit factor at least 1.10. The best
selection expectancy among selectable variants becomes the sole candidate.
Promotion additionally requires at least 40 SPY final trades, positive SPY
expectancy under doubled costs, profit factor at least 1.10, and positive QQQ
and IWM final expectancy.

This is research-only. It cannot submit orders, block production entries, or
change position size.
