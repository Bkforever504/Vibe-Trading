# Fibonacci Confluence v3 Limit-Entry Preregistration

Created after v2 showed that next-bar market entry consumed the gross edge and
before v3 outcomes were computed.

All v2 anchors, ratios, confirmations, stops, targets, costs, periods, and
external checks remain fixed. Only entry mechanics change:

- A completed signal authorizes a limit at the exact retracement level on the
  next bar.
- The trade exists only if that next bar trades through the limit.
- No later-bar chasing and no market-order substitute.
- Require reward-to-risk of at least 1.0.
- Require target distance to be at least three times modeled round-trip cost.

This is still exploratory because v1 and v2 exposed the historical corpus.
Passing would authorize paper shadow comparison only.
