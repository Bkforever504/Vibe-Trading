## Rules required before a future tournament

For any YouTube-derived setup, record all fields before code or ranking changes:

1. Instrument and session, including time zone and eligible timeframes.
2. Exact level construction using only data known at the signal time.
3. Completed-bar entry condition and next-bar execution convention.
4. Initial stop/invalidation and target/exit/time-stop rules.
5. Position and re-entry constraints, plus spread/slippage assumptions.
6. Date-blocked out-of-sample and forward-shadow acceptance criteria.

Until all six are source-supported or explicitly user-specified, the system must label the content `unresolved_social_hypothesis` and must not use it for an alert, ranking, or execution decision.
